StudentVUE connection test

This page only tests whether Grade Lab can sign in to MCPS StudentVUE and read your grades. It does not change anything in Grade Lab.

Where your Student ID and password go

  • They are sent once, over an encrypted HTTPS connection, to Grade Lab's server program studentvue-test in Grade Lab's Supabase project (ibqiwhgvwuizxpwefwqq.supabase.co).
  • That program sends them to one place only: MCPS StudentVUE at md-mcps-psv.edupoint.com, to sign in.
  • Nothing is saved: not in Supabase, not in this browser, not in any log. The boxes are cleared as soon as you press the button.
  • What comes back to this page: two yes/no results, your class names with their current grade, assignment counts, and the names of StudentVUE's data fields. No tokens, no password, no personal details.
  • Your Grade Lab grades and account data are not read or changed.
